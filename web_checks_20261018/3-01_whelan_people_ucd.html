BIO

Karl Whelan has been Professor of Economics at University College Dublin since 2007. Previously, he worked for the Central Bank of Ireland and the Federal Reserve Board in Washington DC. Prof. Whelan has published extensively in the area of applied macroeconomics and is a member of the Royal Irish Academy. He has appeared often in the media discussing economic policy issues and has been an expert witness at Irish parliamentary committees. Professor Whelan also acted as an advisor to the European Parliament's Economic and Monetary Affairs Committee from 2009 to 2024, writing many briefing papers on issures related to monetary policy. In recent years, he has published extensively on the economics of betting and prediction markets. You can find more information on Professor Whelan at his personal website www.karlwhelan.com.
